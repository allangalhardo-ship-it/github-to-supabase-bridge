- Format on-screen numbers, channel names and cash categories with helpers in src/lib/format.ts (fmtNum, formatQuantidadeBR, formatCanalLabel, formatCategoriaCaixa); keep toFixed only for form/DB values — keeps pt-BR display consistent without breaking inputs.
- Sales are orders: `vendas_pedidos` is the order header and `vendas` rows are its items (`pedido_id`). Create/edit through the `salvar_venda_pedido` RPC and cancel through `cancelar_venda_pedido`; a BEFORE INSERT trigger gives any bare `vendas` insert its own order (grouped by `numero_pedido_externo`) and keeps header totals in sync — why: item-level sums keep working while counts, tickets and channel grouping use orders.
- Manual-sale payment previews use the same separated commission/payment rates and receiver as the order RPC; cash views consume `get_caixa_pedidos` and exclude future settlements from realized totals — prevents preview/save and cash timing divergence.
- Channel deletion is guarded by a database trigger in addition to the confirmation UI — preserves sale history even during concurrent requests.
- Cash-page and cash-flow reads share `carregarLinhasCaixaPedidos`, which completes monthly fees only when the deployed RPC omits them — keeps both displays aligned with the balance RPC without duplicate fees.
